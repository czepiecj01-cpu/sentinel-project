01 // Why this matters

The attackers already use AI. The defenders don't.

For the first time in the history of cybersecurity, the barrier to sophisticated attack has collapsed — while the cost of sophisticated defense has not. That gap is where societies are bleeding.

// Threat scale
4 min

An AI-assisted operator can generate 40 personalised spearphishing lures in under four minutes. Ransomware groups already run this workflow at industrial scale.

// Defender gap
71%

Of European hospitals, municipalities, and SMEs cannot afford enterprise-grade cybersecurity talent or tooling. They run legacy systems patched years late.

// Pace of change
12–18

Months between frontier capability and its open-weight equivalent. Today's defensive innovation is tomorrow's offensive tool. Innovation cannot be paused — it must be matched.

One platform. Three agents. Continuous defense.

SENTINEL RED is our autonomous penetration-testing agent — a "good hacker" that probes your systems the way an attacker would, on a continuous loop rather than an annual audit.

SENTINEL BLUE is the counter-agent: an AI defender that monitors traffic, detects anomalies, and neutralises intrusions in real time — including attacks from other AI agents.

SENTINEL ADVISE translates what the agents learn into policy: NIS2 compliance reports, board briefings, staff training, and a chatbot for the non-technical people who keep your organisation running.
